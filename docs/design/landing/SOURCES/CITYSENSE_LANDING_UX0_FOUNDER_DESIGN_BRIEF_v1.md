# CitySense Lab — Landing Page UX-0 Founder Direction Brief

**Date:** 2026-10-11  
**Decision:** Founder SELECTED hybrid visual direction: **A Editorial visual quality + C functional clarity + B urban-intelligence positioning**.  
**Stage status:** Direction selected; high-fidelity design, content, implementation, and deployment **NOT YET APPROVED**.  
**Scope:** Public CitySense landing page to communicate the real early-stage project and support legitimate affiliate-partner applications; future reusable entry into the mobile-first PWA.

## 1. Strategic positioning
CitySense is an emerging **context-aware city intelligence and service-access platform**, beginning with Batumi tourist discovery and informed selection. It is *not* just an affiliate marketplace, general travel blog, map directory, sensor dashboard, or an already-functioning citywide real-time intelligence network.

Narrative hierarchy:
1. **Useful now / first product being developed:** explain a concrete visitor task: express a need → compare up to five well-evidenced alternatives → see the map and evidence → select → use an authorized partner action where genuinely supported.
2. **Why the product is distinctive:** context-aware, practical constraint-first choice; evidence and unknowns; geographic interface. Never promise mathematically best outcomes or service-quality superiority.
3. **Long-term platform direction:** a modular city service registry, verified local supply and partner actions, source-governed spatial/temporal data, and versioned interfaces that could serve external AI agents and additional cities.
4. **Commercial approach:** initially may use authorized affiliate links; then validate owned supplier relationships and agent-usable actions. No unapproved partner marks, bookings or conversion claims.

### Candidate messaging (to be visually tested, not automatically public-approved)
- Eyebrow: `BATUMI FIRST. BUILT FOR CONNECTED CITIES.`
- Headline: `Discover a city with context.`
- Supporting line: `CitySense is developing a more informed way to explore Batumi — connecting places, practical needs and trusted service information. Our longer-term vision is an intelligence layer that people, local businesses and authorized AI agents can use.`
- Status label: `Pilot in development`.
- Primary CTA until there is a real product: `Explore the vision` (scroll to explanation) or `See how it will work` — **not** `Book now`, `Explore live`, `Try AI` or `See availability` without functioning features.
- Secondary CTA: `For potential partners` → factual partnership enquiry using a verified contact channel.
- Future-oriented section title: `From city discovery to city intelligence.`
- Affiliate disclosure: `When available, some outbound partner links may earn CitySense a commission. Recommendations should remain based first on user needs and stated constraints.` Only publish after legal/content review and align actual behavior.

## 2. Visual direction
**A / Editorial:** authentic Batumi photographic imagery, confident typographic hierarchy, spacious composition, visual restraint, editorial sophistication. Avoid generic stock/AI skyline passed off as actual Batumi.
**C / Functional:** clear explanation flow, compact accessible navigation, predictable CTA hierarchy, concise service categories and mobile patterns. Do not depict unsupported live-search/booking.
**B / Platform:** precise restrained diagram or geographic data motif explaining `people ↔ places ↔ services ↔ authorized agents` and modular infrastructure. Do not use dramatic neon 'digital brain', fictitious heatmaps, fabricated real-time pins, or unsupported claims.

### Suggested art direction
- Photography: licensed authentic Batumi coastline/urban-life imagery, legible overlays and controlled color grading; original assets only or properly licensed.
- Typography: contemporary sans serif with Georgian glyph support and intentional hierarchy (English-first, Georgian-ready).
- Palette: warm off-white, charcoal/deep navy, muted coastline blue or sea-glass accent; one controlled accent, not rainbow markers or gratuitous gradients.
- Motion: subtle and optional; honor reduced-motion settings.
- Iconography: consistent line style, functional not decorative.
- Visual differentiation: maps and content coexist; product preview should be clearly marked **conceptual** until actual live product exists.
- Contrast: avoid 'dark sci-fi' throughout. Reserve dark platform section for architectural vision where useful.

## 3. Page structure — first candidate storyboard
1. **Navigation:** logo/wordmark, Vision, How it works, For partners, Contact, EN/KA (only if actual translations provided). Single honest CTA.
2. **Hero:** authentic Batumi image with concise copy and status; teaser of mobile map-first experience **visibly labeled concept preview**.
3. **How it works:** need and constraints → up-to-five relevant alternatives → user-controlled comparison on a map → informed selection; explicit critical evidence gaps.
4. **What starts in Batumi:** tours, activities, travel services, future connectivity, independently justified categories with labels `planned` where applicable. No fictional prices, ratings or claimed availability.
5. **City intelligence roadmap:** diagram with evidence/source layer → need/selection layer → authorized service/agent interface; distinguish existing foundation, pilot goals and aspirational capabilities.
6. **Partner/distribution section:** accommodation QR pilot *proposed/permissions pending*; service providers and airport-counter partnerships *hypotheses only*. Invitation to speak, not assertion of partnerships.
7. **Trust footer:** project stage, owner/contact (real verified details), privacy and terms (reviewed), affiliate disclosure if applicable, data/source limitations, no unauthorized provider logos.

## 4. Required visual concepts & artifacts
Create **one refined hybrid direction**, explored through **three composition options within that direction**, not three unrelated brand strategies:
- Variant H1: Photography-dominant editorial hero + minimal product preview.
- Variant H2: Balanced editorial photography + product map/list illustration, equal hierarchy.
- Variant H3: Product-preview dominant hero + authentic city image + quiet platform vision.

For all three provide:
- Desktop 1440 px and mobile 390 px artboards (320 px stress test planned).
- Above-the-fold and full-page layouts, readable copy and clear navigation.
- Hero, how-it-works, three-state product proof labels, future platform diagram, partnership section and footer.
- Same positioning and honest feature-status language, differentiated mainly by composition, hierarchy and art direction.
- Visual reference board listing source URL, creator/license and **what to borrow / not copy**.
- A11y contrast annotation, typography specs, spacing grid, image rights and localization constraints.
- Mobile viewport behavior, safe areas, tap target adequacy and visual prioritization; no mobile layout reduced to squeezed desktop.
- A visual comparison matrix and one recommendation with uncertainties.

Mockups may use composition placeholders only if conspicuously labeled. **Generated images or concept maps are not evidence of real Batumi maps or shipping product.** Do not invent providers' logos, customer logos, reviews, historical stats, user counts, available offers, features, urban state values, awards, bookings, or partnerships.

## 5. Acceptance criteria — founder gates
Founder separately evaluates:
- Strategic fidelity: tourist wedge and agent-accessible city-intelligence long-term vision in proper hierarchy.
- Professionalism: restrained, original, credible; no generic AI slop.
- Visual hierarchy: clear first read within seconds, usable mobile layout.
- Truthfulness: readiness/claim accuracy and clear conceptual illustration labeling.
- Feasibility: reusable PWA components without making landing-page styling a fixed product architecture.
- Compliance: image and source licenses, attribution, accessible copy/controls.

**UX-0 gate:** founder can `APPROVE DIRECTION`, `REQUEST REVISION`, `REJECT`, or `HOLD`. Selection of direction does NOT approve H1–H3 layouts. Future UX-1 wireframes and UX-2 high-fidelity must each have their own founder gate. No HTML/CSS/React implementation, deployment, domain registration, provider credentials or affiliate registration under this brief.

## 6. Explicit open inputs
- Real contact email, legal publisher identity and jurisdiction details for privacy/terms.
- Real, rights-cleared Batumi imagery.
- Exact language coverage for launch.
- Partner distribution permissions and affiliate approvals.
- Existing Map Stage 1B actual provider render and usage rights (not required to visually explore hero, required before claiming product preview is actual).

## 7. Engineering handoff constraints
Only after founder approves a specific high-fidelity option should Chief Engineer prepare a separate implementation ticket, explicit file ownership, preview QA, privacy/legal copy review, and deployment gate. Landing page must be provider-neutral, accessible, responsive, and future-PWA-compatible; do not create a new booking service or modify the protected research/scoring backend.
