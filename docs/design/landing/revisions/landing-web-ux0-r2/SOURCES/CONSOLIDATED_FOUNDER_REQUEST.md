1. Establish a professional and credible public presence for Viator and other affiliate partner applications.

3. Present the longer-term ambition of city intelligence and agent-accessible service infrastructure.

## 3. Approved positioning

CitySense is NOT simply:

- An affiliate marketplace.
- A tourist catalogue.
- A conventional chatbot.
- A generic smart-city dashboard.
- A booking aggregator.

CitySense is developing toward:

**Context-Aware City Intelligence & Agent-Accessible Local Service Infrastructure.**

The initial real-world use case is contextual tourist discovery in Batumi.

Affiliate programs and third-party commercial APIs are a capital-efficient initial monetization and supply strategy — not the ultimate identity or defensible competitive advantage.

The landing website must express the relationship between today's first tourist use case and the longer-term city intelligence vision, without conflating planned and operational features.

Do not claim partnerships, inventory rights, real-time city conditions, bookings or public agent capabilities that have not been independently verified.

## 4. Revised design direction

Preserve the Founder-selected hybrid design philosophy:

- Editorial visual sophistication and authentic Batumi identity.
- Functional clarity and intuitive information hierarchy.
- Credible, technically grounded urban-intelligence positioning.

Replace the generic startup landing-page treatment with:

**Spatial Intelligence × Conversational Interaction × Progressive Disclosure**

Central design principle:

**Demonstrate the intelligence; do not merely describe it.**

The website's hero should communicate one coherent relationship between:

- A real urban location or recognizable Batumi geographic context.
- A representative visitor need.
- Relevant practical constraints.
- Sources of city/place/service evidence.
- A clear selection or comparison concept.
- An authorized or future action.
- The longer-term possibility of third-party agent access.

This must be an elegant, comprehensible visual explanation rather than a fictional functioning application.

Use conceptual states and annotated transitions where appropriate.

Do not create a complex working map, production chatbot or recommendation engine for this landing website.

## 6. Partners page — new bounded design requirement

Introduce a dedicated public **For Business / Partners** page within LANDING-WEB.

Its objective is to communicate the commercial motivation for local businesses and distribution partners to engage with CitySense.

Develop two distinctly explained partner paths:

**Service Partners**
- Tour and activity operators.
- Private drivers and transfers.
- Paid attractions.
- Sports facilities and other eligible local services.

**Distribution Partners**
- Hotels, apartments and other accommodation providers.
- Potential airport-area commercial distribution partners, subject to their permissions and commercial incentives.

Show a conceptual pathway:

Tourist need → suitability/eligibility → relevant business → permitted contact or request → partner response.

The Partners page must not promise:
- Guaranteed customer acquisition.
- Proven conversion improvements.
- Existing partner commitments.
- Fully working availability synchronization.
- A currently operating partner dashboard.
- Automatic booking/payment infrastructure.

Include proposed Founding Partner participation and an honest expression-of-interest mechanism, but do not build a functional partner portal in this stage.

Prepare:
- Partners page desktop/mobile design concept.
- Partner value proposition and information hierarchy.
- CTA copy.
- Partner onboarding outline.
- Disclosure, legal and contact requirements.
- Relationship to the main landing-page visual system.

Avoid adding a separate Partners page for each of the three directions unless needed for meaningful founder comparison. One coherent proposed Partners page aligned to the leading visual direction is sufficient.
