# Three visual narratives, one truthful scenario

These static LANDING-WEB concepts explain developing **Context-Aware City Intelligence & Agent-Accessible Local Service Infrastructure**.
Batumi tourist discovery is the initial use case. The illustrative need is an outdoor afternoon with essential step-free access.
No place evidence is connected: missing access evidence causes a hold, not an invented recommendation.
Conditional A/B slots explain comparison after eligibility; up to five means fewer or none where warranted.
Permitted future contact, partner response and future authorized agent access remain separate from confirmation and fulfillment.

| Direction | Composition and disclosure | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| S1 Cinematic Spatial Narrative | Large authentic coast scene anchors an editorial quotation; a separate essential-fact column interrupts the story. State sequence uses successive photographed chapters. | Strongest premium location identity; more photographic repetition and greater risk of under-explaining evidence. Explicit hold and stage captions address this. |
| S2 Integrated City Canvas | One bounded canvas unifies city context, representative intent, evidence questions, uncertainty and conditional alternatives. States keep the same context frame while the question changes. | Best balance of explanation and restrained near-term website cost; a conceptual canvas could be mistaken for a working app, so the illustrative label stays prominent. |
| S3 Intelligence in Motion | A numbered horizontal decision trace becomes a vertical mobile spine; future agents appear as a sixth, separately qualified step. State sequence uses typography and ordering instead of repeated photo cards. | Clearest logic and future architecture; less cinematic identity and more reading. Static order remains comprehensible without animation. |

**Recommendation: S2 with the single S2-aligned Partners page.** This is advice, not accepted direction.
Same underlying facts/palette do not make these variants color swaps: focal structure, sequencing and information containment differ.
The [overview](artboards/S-comparison-overview.png) and full-size originals permit direct comparison.

Desktop layout: 1440 wide, 120 side margins, 1200 content width; two 576 columns separated by 48 where relevant.
Mobile: independent 390 compositions, 24 side margins and 342 content width; S1 full-bleed photo is intentional.
S2 canvas has 16 inner padding and 310 photo/text width. Content length is not a fixed viewport target.
Desktop hero headers 48–57; mobile headers 30–34 with 37–40 leading. Body 17–21 desktop and 15–17 mobile;
small provenance/status labels 10–14 are supplementary. CTA design target 48 high. Typography is Segoe UI;
Georgia is an English-only S1 editorial accent. Details and limitations: [accessibility](ACCESSIBILITY_AND_MOBILE.md).

Palette: paper `#F5F2EA`, ink `#172D32`, secondary `#4C6264`, accent `#21675F`, white `#FFFFFF`,
rule `#CDCFC5`, pale surface `#E6EBE3`, missing-fact amber `#74521F`; dark-panel text `#D0DDD6` / `#B9D8C9`.
Uncertainty always has literal text, so amber is not the sole cue. No neon, gradients, fake network or metrics dashboard.

Each direction includes six actual desktop/mobile state panels: need → required fact → evidence boundary → conditional comparison →
permitted future action → future authorized agents. They are an explanation, not a valid product journey or interactive prototype.
The GIFs are six-frame, 1.8-second hard-cut timed storyboards demonstrating order. They do not implement the motion proposed below.

| Direction | Proposed later motion, subject to approval | Reduced-motion alternative already drawn |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Deliberate chapter change with 250–400ms opacity transition; no parallax/geographic animation | Complete stationary chapter storyboard; every qualifier and outcome is visible |
| S2 | 150–250ms emphasis transfer within a stable canvas; no automatic chat or live evidence pulse | Complete stationary canvas states, ordered with text; no timed emphasis needed |
| S3 | 180–240ms step emphasis along the reasoning order; no looping map/network | Complete stationary numbered trace; no automatic reveal |

Durations/easing are proposals, not measured browser behavior. Future implementation should use explicit user progression,
avoid required timed disclosure/autoplay, and honor reduced motion. All required information survives without motion.
No performance, keyboard, screen-reader or device claim follows from raster artwork.

Partners uses the S2 type, surfaces and explanatory hierarchy; service/distribution paths remain distinct.
IA is deliberately small: Home / For Partners / verified Contact; policies in footer, vision inside Home.
Affiliate supply is an initial strategy and does not define the identity. No layout/component/color/state specification automatically
transfers to PRODUCT-APP. Shared brand is only proposed; the product keeps independent UX-0–4 approvals.
