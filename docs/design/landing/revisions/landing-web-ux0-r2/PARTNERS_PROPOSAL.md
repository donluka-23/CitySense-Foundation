# For Partners — one S2-aligned concept

Goal: explain why service and distribution businesses might explore participation, without asserting an operating marketplace or guaranteed acquisition.
Hero: **Local services, understood in context.** Developing contextual discovery in Batumi; participation is being explored.
Editorial two-path hierarchy → proposed value path → Founding Partner enquiry concept → onboarding outline → future agent vision → truthful footer.

| Path | Proposed motivation | Scope and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Service Partners | Present the practical facts that help visitors decide whether a service meets their needs before contact | Tours/activities, paid attractions, sports and eligible local services. Drivers/transfers may express prospective interest; operations remain deferred and separately authorized. No approved listing, leads, scores or availability promised. |
| Distribution Partners | Explore a permission-based introduction to contextual discovery for guests | Hotels/apartments/accommodation; possible airport-area commercial touchpoints subject to permission and agreed incentives. No QR placement, airport endorsement, incentive amounts or conversion proof. |

Proposed pathway: visitor need → eligibility before suitability → relevant business → permitted contact/request → partner response.
Permissions/evidence precede an action; a request and response do not establish booking confirmation or fulfillment.
No working dashboard, synchronization, checkout, payment, automated booking or customer-acquisition guarantee is represented.

Founding Partner participation is proposed. Scope/commercial terms and actual selection would be agreed individually.
Candidate CTA: **Express interest in becoming a Founding Partner**; condensed artwork CTA **Express interest · proposed**.
Adjacent notice: static form concept; verified contact channel pending; no information submitted or collected.
EOI proposal: business name, Service/Distribution path, one preferred reply channel, optional short description.
Do not ask for customer data, IDs, payment details, credentials or a mandatory phone-and-email pair.
An enquiry would not be a partnership agreement, accepted membership, listing or booking.

Later onboarding outline: introduction → identity/authority/permissions → evidence/contact/content rights → participation terms → separately authorized bounded test.
Distribution adds placement/removal/referral permissions; services add category-specific facts/service area and responsibilities.
Initial EOI is not document verification or an operating onboarding workflow.

Future authorized-agent discovery is a distinct lower-page vision. It shares governed evidence/permissions in the intended architecture,
not public callable tools or partner API activation. LANDING-WEB layouts create no PRODUCT-APP specification.

Publication inputs: actual publisher/jurisdiction/verified destination; recipients, purpose, retention/deletion and privacy information
matched to the eventual contact route; truthful terms and authorized affiliate relationship/disclosure; any partner/logo/data rights.
Marketing opt-in, if ever proposed, requires its own clear choice. Partner enquiries must not flow into immutable research stores by implication.
CF-02 remains unresolved: no paid priority/boosted ranking or quality advantage is offered.

Official affiliate research: [evidence and uncertainty](CLAIMS_AND_RELEASE_INPUTS.md). Professional website credibility is the Founder goal;
no provider acceptance, traffic threshold, mandatory page count or affiliate-account approval is invented.
