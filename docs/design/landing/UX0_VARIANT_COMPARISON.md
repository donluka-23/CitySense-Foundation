# Three compositions of the selected hybrid

Actual [comparison overview](artboards/UX0-comparison-overview.png); full-size [artifact index](README.md).
Judgments below are design review, not measured conversion, user testing or Founder acceptance.

| Dimension | H1 Editorial First | H2 Balanced Experience | H3 Product First |
|---|---|---|---|
| First read | Batumi atmosphere and confident editorial headline | Tourist purpose alongside city/photo and proposed interface | Need, constraints and evidence structure lead |
| Desktop | Broad coast photo, opaque headline panel, restrained preview below | Left value proposition, right photo then labeled map/list concept | Broad product concept and narrow actual city-photo inset |
| Mobile | Serif lead, full-bleed photo, preview later | Sans lead, inset photo, readable preview after it | Preview before city photo; same calm brand |
| Tourist task clarity | Explained before/after photography; lower immediate mechanics | Strong purpose/preview balance | Strongest immediate conceptual mechanics |
| City intelligence | Quiet separate future section | Integrated story with equally visible future section | Strong data/interface emphasis without dashboard metrics |
| Main risk | May resemble travel editorial; concept preview below first fold | Mobile preview starts near first fold and continues below; long narrative | Placeholder is deliberately abstract; can feel less vivid than a real map |
| Claims risk | Low with status and proper photography credits | Clear map labels and unknown eligibility essential | Highest risk of apparent live product; labels must survive all crops |
| Future reuse | Editorial launch/story material | Strongest combined landing / mobile entry hierarchy | Useful future request/evidence communication, not approved app design |

Chief recommends **H2 for Founder inspection**: one clear visitor task, authentic city identity and readable planned product mechanics,
with a distinct future-platform story. Product/Research and Mobile reviewers independently preferred this balance before artifact generation.
H1 is strongest for place/story emphasis; H3 for the proposed decision experience. None has validated usability or conversion advantage.
The H1 font accent and all three long-page narratives are explicit review choices. The source's contemporary sans-serif preference
is preserved as the shared interface font; Founder may request removing the optional serif accent.

Acceptance uncertainties: 390px artwork is not a physical device; no 320px stress/large-text/browser test was run.
Georgian translations/native review, verified contact/legal identity, lawful eligible inventory and actual provider cartography remain unresolved.
Static labels and token contrasts have been checked; a future working implementation needs separate semantic/accessibility testing.

Founder choices: APPROVE DIRECTION for a named concept and revision; REQUEST REVISION with specific changes; REJECT; HOLD.
A named layout decision alone does not grant implementation or another stage.
