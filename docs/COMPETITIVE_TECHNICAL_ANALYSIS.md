# Competitive technical analysis — checked 2026-10-09

Scope: focused public-source review, no accounts, paid access, outreach or runtime product tests.
**Fact** below means observable publication/access evidence. **Claim** means vendor-reported
capability or performance. **Inference** is CitySense's interpretation. **Unknown** is not evidence
of absence. Public API/MCP descriptions are not proof of deployability, validation or market traction.

## Differentiation hypothesis and evidence gap

The defensibility candidate is independently labeled, consented and reproducible evidence for a
narrow human-need/place/time task, with lawful spatial/environmental lineage and explicit abstention.
No moat exists merely from wrappers, deterministic weights, a local source list or absent competitor
documentation. All competitors' current methods and comparative performance would require deeper
review/access. CitySense has no independent efficacy, customer demand or cross-city transfer evidence.
Next comparison is a matched genuine strong AI+Maps run and blinded field labels, not marketing copy.
