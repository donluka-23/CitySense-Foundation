# Project charter

Approved direction: [Blueprint v0.4](../product/CITYSENSE_PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT_v0_4.md).
Current product/process version adoption is recorded in [VERSION_REGISTER](VERSION_REGISTER.md).
User authorized adoption and this governance bootstrap on 2026-10-10.
The original blueprint's unresolved statuses remain unresolved.

CitySense helps a tourist in Batumi turn an explicit need and essential constraints into a
credible, informed selection through mobile QR entry, comparison and a synchronized map.
The November 30, 2026 target is a supervised closed pilot, contingent on evidence, permissions,
scope and team capacity. Thirty apartments provide a distribution opportunity, not a cohort.

## Narrow pilot outcome

QR/open → need → clarify essential constraints → up to five lawful, defensible alternatives
(or fewer/abstention) → compare/refine → selected place/entrance focus and evidence panel → informed selection.
Routes, contacts and partner requests appear only where separately verified and authorized.
Natural-language input is direction; no approved parsing/model implementation exists.

Known hard constraints precede soft ranking. Unknown required facts do not establish validity.
Sources, freshness, status and caveats remain visible. Do not make empirical accuracy or superiority claims.
V-001/V-006 establish the product outcome; V-002–V-004 metrics/instruments remain proposed.
Keep protected experienced-suitability research and A1/B1 study separate.

## Architecture direction and scope

Preserve the modular monolith, deterministic core, immutable research snapshots and shared service.
PWA, Telegram and later machine clients reuse governed interfaces. Existing private command/demo
and map harnesses are evidence of components, not an integrated tourist product.
Versioned additive boundaries can accommodate place-based, service-area, digital and contact offerings.
Provider adapters cannot bypass source rights, consent, booking authority or suitability policy.

Tours, paid attractions and sports courts are initial commercial segments. November tourist value
must stand alone without offers. A verified information/contact option and any manual request workflow
require distinct readiness gates; payment, eSIM activation, transport reservations, external agent
writes and multi-city rollout are deferred.

## Governance

Founder owns material product, scientific, commercial, privacy, vendor and release decisions.
Chief owns dependency/ownership assignment and integration, subject to accepted contracts.
Logical roles do not create persistent autonomous teams. Implementers do not self-approve.
Use [precedence](DECISION_REGISTER.md), [conflict gates](RECONCILIATION.md) and
[release criteria](RELEASE_GATES.md). No source code, contract or accepted ADR is changed by this charter.

P-005–007 add approved sequential stages, actual visual checkpoint approval and controlled idea intake.
[STAGE_GATES](STAGE_GATES.md) and [FOUNDER_APPROVALS](FOUNDER_APPROVALS.md) govern permission.
These controls preserve the full product goal without expanding the November slice or amending technical contracts.
